# Cisco communicatie in het Engels

Cisco Networking Academy Cup – in collaboration with Real Madrid

The Networking Academy Cup is a three-stage student competition designed to inspire students and instructors in the fields of cybersecurity and networking.

The ultimate goal is to reach the final stage — an unforgettable Capture the Flag (CTF) event bringing together participants from Europe, the Middle East, and Africa.

For guidance on how to best prepare your students, please contact bob@biasc.be or yvan@biasc.be.

# Why Participate?
- Engage your students in a real-world cybersecurity and networking challenge.
- Compete with teams from across the EMEA region.
- Learn from top experts and gain international visibility.
- Win prizes, including an unforgettable experience in Madrid.
    

All Cisco Networking Academy instructors are encouraged to attend the webinar to discover how their students can take part and benefit from this high-level learning experience.

Cisco Networking Academy has officially opened registration for the EMEA NetAcad Cup 2025, an international competition organized in collaboration with Real Madrid FC.

This is a unique opportunity for students from across Europe, the Middle East, and Africa to showcase their networking and cybersecurity expertise while competing for exclusive prizes — including a visit to Madrid and the legendary Real Madrid facilities. The final stage promises to be an exhilarating, high-energy experience — a rare chance to put real-time skills to the test in a friendly international competition among top student teams from the entire EMEA region.
